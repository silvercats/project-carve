PROJECT CARVE WEBSITE
=====================

Preview on your computer
------------------------
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the Project_Carve_Website folder.
3. Double-click index.html.

Publish on Netlify
------------------
1. Sign in to Netlify.
2. Open Sites and choose "Add new site" > "Deploy manually".
3. Drag the extracted Project_Carve_Website folder into the upload area.

Important
---------
- Keep index.html, transformation.mp4, transformation-poster.jpg, and
  favicon.svg together in the same folder.
- WhatsApp buttons currently point to 070 686 6700.
- The displayed program price is Rs. 210,000.
