STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions you should ask before joining.

Still unsure about something?

Ask Haritha directly
Where do the training sessions happen?+

At your gym, at home, or at a selected partner gym in Colombo. We agree on the most practical option during the fit check. Gym membership and venue fees are not included.

How much time does this take each week?+

Usually two supervised training sessions of about an hour each, any additional solo training your track calls for, and a short weekly progress review. The plan is designed around your schedule.

Do I need supplements or fat burners?+

No. Project Carve is built around real food and structured training. Haritha's own 30kg transformation used no supplements or fat burners.

Will I lose 30kg in three months too?+

That was Haritha's personal result over a longer period, not a three-month promise. Your pace depends on your starting point, health, consistency and circumstances. The promise is an honest, structured process — not a made-up number by day 90.

What happens after the three months?+

You leave knowing how to manage your nutrition, training and progress reviews yourself. Maintenance coaching is available, but the program is built from day one to reduce dependence on a coach.

What if Project Carve is not right for me?+

The WhatsApp fit check is designed to find that out before you commit. If Haritha does not believe the program fits your needs, he will tell you directly. There is no lock-in contract.
