Where do the sessions happen?

At your gym, at home, or at a selected partner gym in Colombo. If you'd rather train somewhere private, home sessions are an option. Gym membership and venue fees are not included.

How much time does it take each week?

Usually two 1:1 sessions of about an hour, any solo training your pace calls for, and a short weekly review. The plan is built around your schedule.

Do I have to give up rice and curry?

No. You learn portions and meal structure that fit the way you already eat, instead of cutting out the foods you live with.

I have a medical condition or an injury. Can I join?

Tell me about it in the fit check, so the plan can be built around it. Project Carve is coaching, not medical treatment: get your doctor's go-ahead before you start.

How does payment work? Can I get a refund?

The full Rs. 210,000 is paid once, before your first session, and it isn't refundable, including if you stop early. That's why the fit check and the Rs. 5,000 assessment come first: you'll know exactly how it would work for you before you pay. If you join, the assessment fee is credited.

What happens after the three months?

You leave knowing how to manage your food, training and reviews yourself. Maintenance coaching is available if you want it, but the program is built to make you independent.
