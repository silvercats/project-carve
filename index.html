STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions you should ask before joining.

Still unsure about something?

Ask Haritha directly
Where do the training sessions happen?+

At your gym, at home, or at a selected partner gym in Colombo. If you'd rather train somewhere private, home sessions are an option. We agree on the most practical place during the fit check and assessment. Gym membership and venue fees are not included.

How much time does this take each week?+

Usually two supervised training sessions of about an hour each, any additional solo training your track calls for, and a short weekly progress review. The plan is designed around your schedule.

Do I have to give up rice and curry?+

No. Project Carve is built on real food, including rice and curry. You learn portions and meal structure that fit the way you already eat, instead of cutting out the foods you live with.

Do I need supplements or fat burners?+

No. Project Carve is built around real food and structured training. Haritha's own 30kg transformation used no supplements or fat burners.

Will I lose 30kg in three months too?+

That was Haritha's personal result over a longer period, not a three-month promise. Your pace depends on your starting point, health, consistency and circumstances. The promise is an honest, structured process — not a made-up number by day 90.

I have a medical condition or an injury. Can I join?+

Tell Haritha about it in the fit check, so the plan can be built around it. Project Carve is coaching, not medical treatment: if you have a medical condition or an injury, get your doctor's go-ahead before you start.

What if I travel or have a busy week?+

Busy weeks and travel are expected. Tell Haritha early and your plan for that week is adjusted, so one disrupted week doesn't undo your progress.

How does payment work? Can I get a refund?+

The full Rs. 210,000 is paid once, before your first session, and it isn't refundable, including if you decide to stop early. That's why the fit check and the Rs. 5,000 assessment come first: you'll know exactly how the program would work for you before you pay. If you join, the assessment fee is credited, so the total stays Rs. 210,000.

What happens after the three months?+

You leave knowing how to manage your nutrition, training and progress reviews yourself. Maintenance coaching is available, but the program is built from day one to reduce dependence on a coach.

What if Project Carve is not right for me?+

The fit check and the assessment are designed to find that out before you pay for the program. If Haritha does not believe the program fits your needs, you'll be told directly.
